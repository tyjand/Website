Stakeholder Mapping

Overview

Aiming to understand how environmental conditions impact surrounding stakeholders, this project mapped the relationships between a deteriorating crosswalk and related programs, infrastructure, and governing bodies in Ann Arbor. It taught me how to visualize complex relationships and shifted my perspective on research methods, showing how seemingly obvious issues can reveal deeper systemic connections.
