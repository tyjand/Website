Crosswalk Analysis

Overview

Crosswalks are often overlooked as simple infrastructure, yet they profoundly shape how users interact with urban environments. This project explored crosswalks as a key accessibility feature, framing them as a "modern-day trolley problem" in mobility prioritization. Through this lens, we sought to understand how crosswalks contribute to building harmony within tense and diverse communities.
