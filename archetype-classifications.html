Archetype Classifications

Overview

Utilizing Figma, this project applied ethnographic research concepts to develop and classify archetypes within a specific user group. My team focused on crosswalk waiting behavior, iterating on a classification system through methods such as affinity mapping, card sorting, and behavioral analysis. These research tools helped us translate observations into a credible and refined deliverable.
